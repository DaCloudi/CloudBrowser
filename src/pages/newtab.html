You’re incognito

Nothing you do in this window is saved on this computer. When you close all incognito windows, CloudBrowser forgets:

  • the pages you visited and what you searched for
  • cookies and site data, so you’re signed out of everything
  • anything you typed into forms, and site permissions

Downloads and bookmarks you make are kept. Websites, your employer or school, and your internet provider can still see what you do.
