Good to know

Most extensions work, but CloudBrowser isn’t Chrome, so a few features inside some extensions may not. Extensions don’t run in incognito windows.

VPN extensions (like Proton VPN) can’t work here, because CloudBrowser can’t let extensions control its connection. Use the Proton VPN app for Windows instead — it protects CloudBrowser and everything else on your computer.
