What to skip

VR

VR mode
When SteamVR, Quest Link, Virtual Desktop or VRChat is running, CloudBrowser puts unused tabs to sleep after 2 minutes so your headset gets the power.
Interface size
Bigger tabs, buttons and text, easier to read and hit in a VR desktop view.
Pop-out windows
Right-click a tab and choose “Pop out” to float it on top of everything, handy for guides or videos while you play. Close the floating window to put it back.
