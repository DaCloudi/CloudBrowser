Desktop mode

Controls in desktop mode
  • Trigger clicks, A or X right-clicks, the thumbstick scrolls.
  • Hold grip while pointing at a screen to grab it and move it.
  • Click the right thumbstick to bring your screens back in front of you.
  • Press ☰ once for the Cloud Desktop menu: recenter, keyboard, bigger or smaller, closer or further, curve, room view, and leave desktop mode.

Wi-Fi tips

    Microphone and sound

    VB-Cable needed
    Windows needs a free virtual microphone for this. Cloud Desktop downloads VB-Cable from its maker and opens the installer: click Yes, then “Install Driver”, then restart your PC.
    Test the mic
    While your headset is connected, click Test and talk. The bar moves when the PC hears you.

    USB cable

    Cable checker
    Plug your Quest into this PC and find out if the cable and port are fast enough: USB 2 or 3, and whether it charges your headset. It only asks the headset; nothing heavy goes down the cable.

      Your Quest
      Plug your Quest into this PC with a USB cable. Developer Mode must be on (Meta Horizon app on your phone › Devices › Headset settings › Developer Mode). Afterwards it’s in App Library › Unknown Sources.
      Cloud Boost (for Quest games)
      A Quest app that makes standalone games faster or sharper: resolution, refresh rate, CPU and GPU level, foveation. Plug your Quest in and click Set up Cloud Boost once: it installs the app and gets the headset’s permission (you tap Allow in the headset). After that it works without the PC.
